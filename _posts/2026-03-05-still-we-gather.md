My piece Budding Abolitionist showed at Rosebud Gallery as part of the Still We Gather show from March 5th to 27th 2026.

The American myth of belonging, that everyone is welcome here, has fallen apart with rise of Christian nationalism. As an artist who is both fascinated by fairy tales and horrified by the very real terror inflicted on immigrant children in the US, I created “Budding Abolitionist." The children's book illustration style disguises a hidden message much like those often found in fairy tales.

All my personal proceeds from the sale of “Budding Abolitionist” will go to the Minnesota ACLU.
